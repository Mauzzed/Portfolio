Flagship Project: AstraCare Companion

A patient-facing symptom tracking and oncology care coordination mobile application engineered for oncology patients undergoing complex chemotherapy cycles.

  • Symptom Gradient Sliders: Intuitive 1–10 tactile sliders with localized severity labels (Mild, Moderate, High) designed for quick logging during peak treatment fatigue.
  • Treatment Milestone Trackers: Visual linear roadmap tracking chemotherapy cycles, pre-surgery consultations, and upcoming debulking procedures.
  • Clinical Liaison View: Dual-sided architecture allowing patients to log symptoms while oncologists receive standardized clinical reports.
